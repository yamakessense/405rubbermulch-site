The real problem

You're not paying for mulch. You're paying for a truck.

Rubber mulch is expensive to move. A single 2,000-lb supersack is too heavy and too awkward to ship on its own at any price that makes sense — national suppliers know it, which is why a lot of them quote $1,000 or more delivered for what should be a $425 product. You're paying for a truck to make a special trip for one pallet.

We solve it differently: we run full truckloads — 22 supersacks at a time — which is the only way the freight math works out to $425 a sack. The catch? Not every playground needs 22 sacks. So instead of you paying a partial-load penalty, we help your town fill the truck: your playground's sacks, plus the extras your raffle and flowerbed sponsorships put to work. Everybody gets the truckload price because the truckload actually gets full.

It happens all the time: businesses have split a truckload. Churches have split loads. Once a truck is coming to town, a supersack of mulch becomes something everybody wants a piece of — your neighbors will help you empty that flatbed.

Two ways towns are doing this right now

The raffle isn't bolted onto the mulch sale. It's what fills the truck.

These aren't just fundraisers — they're the mechanism that makes $425 delivered possible for a town that only needs eight sacks.

The Mulch Raffle

Sell tickets. Fund the playground. Somebody wins a yard of mulch.

Sell raffle tickets for a prize like a free supersack delivered to the winner's yard — or a full mulch-and-install bundle. All ticket money goes straight to the playground fund. It's simple, it's fun, and it gives your town a reason to talk about the project three or four times before the drawing.

From the Fundraising Kit — raffle flyer WIN A FREE YARD OF RUBBER MULCH Delivered right to your driveway — a $425 value. Tickets: $5 each or 5 for $20. All proceeds go to the Playground Fund. Drawing held [date] at [location].
Sponsor-a-Flowerbed

Kids mulch the flowerbeds. Sponsors fund the playground.

Kids and volunteers mulch a local homeowner's or business's flowerbed using leftover supersacks, and the owner makes a sponsorship donation to the playground fund instead of paying a landscaper. Businesses get a sponsor sign at the new playground; homeowners get a fresh flowerbed for the season.

From the Fundraising Kit — sponsorship flyer SPONSOR A FLOWERBED, FUND A PLAYGROUND Our kids will mulch your flowerbed — you help fund the new [Town] playground. Residential: $50. Business: $150 (includes a sponsor sign at the new playground). Contact [name/number] to schedule.

How to get started

The whole plan fits on a napkin.

Tell us your playground's size and equipment height and we'll tell you exactly how many supersacks you need — plus how many extra makes sense for a raffle or sponsorship run.

Step 1 · Order Size it and book the truck One supersack covers 150 sq ft at 6" playground depth, or 300 sq ft at 3" for flowerbeds. Order 2–3 weeks ahead of your install day.
Step 2 · Fundraise Run the raffle & sign-ups Sell raffle tickets and book flowerbed sponsors while the truck's on the calendar. Every extra sack spoken for is money in the playground fund.
Step 3 · Install day Meet the truck, make it a community day Someone in town has a tractor or forklift to pull pallets off the flatbed. Put a tarp down, grab wheelbarrows, slice the sacks open and spread. Extra sacks go to sponsored flowerbeds and the raffle winner.
Step 4 · Photos Show off the new playground Sponsor signs go up, the town paper gets its picture, and the kids get a playground that meets fall-height codes — paid for locally.

Fundraiser FAQ

Questions towns ask us.

How do I get rubber mulch to help fundraise for our playground?

Order supersacks at $425 each, delivered at truckload pricing — a full truck covers roughly 3,300 sq ft at 6" playground depth. Run a raffle or sponsorship drive, and the proceeds go to your playground fund.

Why is your delivered price so much lower than other suppliers?

Because we deliver full truckloads instead of single pallets. A lone supersack is too heavy to ship economically at any price — that's why national suppliers often quote $1,000 or more delivered for it. We fill a full truckload (22 supersacks) by combining your playground's order with your town's raffle or sponsorship sacks, which is what gets everyone down to $425 delivered instead of paying a single-pallet freight penalty.

Can 405 Rubber Mulch help pay for our playground remodel?

Yes — flat delivered pricing plus a raffle or sponsorship plan. Many small towns fund their whole remodel this way, without a grant or a bond issue.

What are good sponsorship ideas for a playground fundraiser?

A mulch raffle, or flowerbed sponsorships where kids mulch a local yard or business frontage in exchange for a donation to the playground fund. Businesses get a sponsor sign at the new playground.

How much does playground rubber mulch cost, delivered?

$425 per 2,000-lb supersack, delivered, at full truckload pricing. A full 22-sack truck is $9,350 flat.

Do I need a quote to order?

No — $425 per supersack delivered is the price at a full truckload. Call (405) 206-8111 or order direct.

How do small, rural towns afford a full playground remodel?

A local raffle or sponsorship drive instead of a grant or bond — the remodel gets funded through community involvement, and the fundraiser itself is what fills the truck to unlock $425 pricing.

Is this the same mulch schools and municipalities are required to use?

Yes — every supersack is tested to ASTM F1292 impact-attenuation standards and meets IPEMA guidance for playground fall-height protection at the depths schools and municipalities are required to install.
