About 405 Rubber Mulch

From a home improvement warehouse to a truckload at a time.

405 Rubber Mulch grew out of Jameson's Discount Home Improvement Warehouse in Oklahoma City. We'd been selling rubber mulch by the bag and by the pallet for years when schools, cities, and landscaping contractors started asking the same question: "Can you just deliver a truckload?"

No paint. No fade.

Why our unpainted black stays black.

Our rubber is cut exclusively from semi-truck tires, not passenger tires. When passenger tires get recycled, their painted and coated whitewalls end up mixed into the rubber stream — that paint is where a lot of "black" rubber mulch actually gets its color. It's also what fades, chalks, and rubs off onto hands, shoes, and clothes over time.

Semi-truck tires don't have whitewalls. There's no paint to begin with — the black is the rubber itself. Our mulch doesn't fade in the sun, doesn't bleed color in the rain, and looks the same five years in as it did the day it was installed. That's why unpainted black is our most popular color.

Every supersack we ship — 2,000 lb, palletized, ready to come off the truck with a forklift or tractor forks — is tested to ASTM F1292 impact-attenuation standards, meeting IPEMA guidance for playground fall-height protection at the depths schools and municipalities are required to install.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

How do I get rubber mulch to help fundraise for our playground?

Order supersacks at $425 each, delivered at truckload pricing — a full truck covers roughly 3,300 sq ft at 6" playground depth. Run a raffle or sponsorship drive, and the proceeds go to your playground fund.

Why is your delivered price so much lower than other suppliers?

Because we deliver full truckloads instead of single pallets. A lone supersack is too heavy to ship economically at any price — that's why national suppliers often quote $1,000 or more delivered for it. We fill a full truckload (22 supersacks) by combining your playground's order with your town's raffle or sponsorship sacks, which is what gets everyone down to $425 delivered instead of paying a single-pallet freight penalty. And ordering less than a full truck never triggers a “7-pallet penalty” here — partial-load pricing scales honestly, and your per-sack price drops with every sack you add.

Can 405 Rubber Mulch help pay for our playground remodel?

Yes — flat delivered pricing plus a raffle or sponsorship plan. Many small towns fund their whole remodel this way, without a grant or a bond issue.

What are good sponsorship ideas for a playground fundraiser?

A mulch raffle, or flowerbed sponsorships where volunteers mulch a local yard or business frontage in exchange for a donation to the playground fund. Businesses get a sponsor sign at the new playground.

How much does playground rubber mulch cost, delivered?

$425 per 2,000-lb supersack, delivered, at full truckload pricing. A full 22-sack truck is $9,350 flat.

Do I need a quote to order?

No — $425 per supersack delivered is the price at a full truckload. Call (405) 206-8111 or order direct.

How do small, rural towns afford a full playground remodel?

A local raffle or sponsorship drive instead of a grant or bond — the remodel gets funded through community involvement, and the fundraiser itself is what fills the truck to unlock $425 pricing.

Can I pay online?

We can arrange payment when we confirm your order. We'll be in close communication before scheduling delivery — covering payment, timing, and what you'll need on site to unload the truck.

How long does it take to receive the truck?

Normally we deliver in less than five days. At peak season it can take a little longer, but we process orders diligently in the order they're received.

Do you sell playground border or underlayment?

No — we sell stripped-down-priced, bulk, playground-certified mulch. Anything else we could sell you wouldn't be true to our roots of saving our customers money.

Is rubber mulch bad for the environment?

We don't believe so. Every truckload keeps tires out of mosquito-breeding, fire-hazard dump sites — in fact, a lot of our mulch is produced from cleaning up illegal tire dumps. We've been in the circular economy for over 10 years: refurbished and repaired discount products, high-quality recycled Vision Quality paint, and a closeout, DIY-and-save approach.

What if I only need a few bags?

If you're local to the OKC metro, stop by either Jameson's store — we're open six days a week. If you're out of range and only need a small amount, we'll show you the math on when it makes sense and when it doesn't for us to supply it. We're famously bad salespeople — we want to save you money, and we want you happy with your purchase.

How long does it take to get a quote?

Not long at all — usually the same day. Heads up: some K-12 and organization-managed inboxes filter our personally sent quotes to spam or block them at the server level. If you're concerned about receiving it, consider which email address you use — or email us directly at b2b@discountokc.com.

Is this the same mulch schools and municipalities are required to use?

Yes — all of our mulch is playground certified. One thing that matters just as much: depth. Certified mulch installed too shallow won't protect the fall height, so tell us your equipment and we'll spec the right depth with your quote.

Is all rubber mulch certified?

Not even close. The equipment to extract the steel and remanufacture tires into certified-safe mulch takes millions of dollars of infrastructure, plus ongoing certification and quality control — and most tire processors don't pursue it. Their tire shred may pass for mulch, but it isn't certified safe. We can't stress this enough: proceed with caution before putting uncertified shred on a playground — you shift all the liability onto yourself.

Can we mix and match colors?

Yes — we build the truck however you like. Unpainted black is standard; painted brown and red run an additional $115 a sack. Black for the playground, brown for the flowerbeds, red for the ball fields — your call.
